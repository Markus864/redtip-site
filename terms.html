New Front Desk clients can try it for 30 days after go-live. At day 30, we compare it with your old voicemail. If it did not answer and book better than your voicemail did, you do not pay the first month's fee. The setup terms in your quote still apply.

Our honest-AI rules apply on every system we build: if a caller asks whether they are talking to AI, it says yes. Calls that are recorded play a recording notice.
