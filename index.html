What we put in writing

The usual complaints about AI phone companies are surprise bills, no way to reach a person, and nobody around after setup. So these go in the agreement.

  1. A person in one step. Anyone who asks for a human gets transferred or gets a confirmed callback time.
  2. Tested before your customers hear it. 50 test calls before launch: angry callers, bad lines, Spanish, emergencies, price shoppers. You get the recordings.
  3. Bookings proven. We book, change and cancel real test appointments in your calendar before go-live.
  4. Watched every week. Every call reviewed weekly for the first month and spot-checked after that. Every fix gets logged.
  5. A flat bill. Spam is free. You get a heads-up at 80% of your minutes. No overage without your yes.
  6. You own it. Month-to-month after setup. Your number, data, reviews, site and content. Leave anytime and get an export and help moving your number within 5 business days.
  7. Fast fixes. Front desk down? Same-day response. Anything else gets a reply within one business day. You deal with Markus directly.
  8. Honest AI. If a caller asks whether it's AI, it says yes. No fake reviews or made-up testimonials, ever.

Questions owners ask

Will my customers know they're talking to AI?

It answers in your business name, in a natural voice, using your prices, hours and policies. If someone asks whether it's AI, it says yes. Most callers just want the job booked.

Can a caller reach a real person?

Yes, anytime. It transfers to you or your on-call tech, or books a confirmed callback time. Emergencies like no heat or an active leak ring a live phone first.

What if it gets something wrong?

It only answers from information you approve. Anything outside that, it takes a message and flags it for you. You see a summary of every conversation, and we review every call with you in the first month.

Does it speak Spanish?

Yes. English and Spanish come with every plan, and it switches when the caller does.

Can it sound like me?

If you want. With your recorded permission we can clone your voice for the phone, your videos and your ads. Or pick a stock voice that fits your business.

Do I have to switch software?

Usually not. We connect to the phone line, calendar, CRM, field software and store you already use, and everything stays in your own accounts.

What about spam calls and going over my minutes?

Spam, robocalls and wrong numbers never count. We warn you at 80% of your minutes, and nothing goes over your plan unless you say yes.

How soon is it running?

Most builds go live 7 to 14 days after the first call, depending on how many tools we connect.

What does it cost?

The Front Desk starts at $500 setup and $297 a month. Each package has a one-time setup fee and one flat monthly fee for hosting, monitoring, fixes and tuning, and you get a fixed quote on the call before anything is built.

Who owns my data, and can I cancel?

You own your number, customer data, reviews, site and content. Plans are month-to-month after setup. If you leave, you get an export and help moving your number within 5 business days.

Do you work with agencies?

Yes. We build and run any of these under your agency's brand at a wholesale price and bill the agency directly.
